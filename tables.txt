icker_24hr: a one-line summary of the last 24 hours for a coin: latest price, highest, lowest, total volume and % change.
klines: a summary of each minute, called a candle: opening price, highest, lowest, closing price and volume for that minute.
aggTrades: the list of actual deals, each with a price, quantity, time and whether the buyer or the seller started it.
bookTicker: the best price a buyer offers (bid) and the best price a seller wants (ask) right now.
